About

I'm Chandini Sushma, a UX/UI designer working across mobile apps, interaction design, and visual exploration. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the edge of what a designer usually gets asked to solve — I care as much about how something feels to use as how it looks.

My work spans product design for mobile platforms, motion and micro-interaction studies, and research-driven design methods — including co-authoring a published study on using child-generated personas to design across cultures.
